Ugandan women who held public office, and which of them Wikipedia has written about

Wikidata records 493 women in Ugandan public office: in parliament, the cabinet, the courts, the civil service, the diplomatic service and local government. 376 have an article somewhere. 117 have none in any language. Only 3 need translating into Luganda, which is the finding that should redirect the work.

Office comes from Wikidata P39, not from job title. A woman counts here because a position is recorded against her, and seniority is read from the position's label. Academic, corporate and ceremonial roles are excluded, which is why 78 more women with a P39 of some kind sit outside the count and are marked “Not a public office” in the list below.

Method. Two Wikidata retrieval branches are unioned: women with Ugandan citizenship who have any P39 position, and women holding a position whose jurisdiction or country is Uganda, which catches office holders with no citizenship statement. Seniority is assigned by matching the position label against ordered keyword lists in src_data/config.py, so “Justice Minister of Uganda” reads as cabinet rather than as a court, and “permanent representative” as a diplomat rather than an MP. The matching is approximate and the label lists are visible in that file.

What this cannot tell you. Wikidata's record of who held which Ugandan office is itself incomplete, so absence here is not proof that a woman never held office. Term dates are recorded for only a minority of positions, so the page does not separate current holders from former ones.
