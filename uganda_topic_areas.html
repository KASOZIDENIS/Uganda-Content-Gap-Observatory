Uganda content gap analysis · cycle 2026-09

What Uganda’s readers look for, and what Luganda does not have

Last year’s analysis ranked 1,194 Uganda articles on English Wikipedia by how much they are read, and published a top ten per topic area. It never asked whether any of those topics exist in Uganda’s own language. 81% of the readership it measured, once the collection is corrected, is on topics with no Luganda article.

Their topic areas, our coverage. The eleven areas and the view figures come from the previous cycle’s exports in training data/, which is the one thing this project had no equivalent of: a reader-facing topic axis rather than a Wikidata type. Everything about coverage comes from this cycle’s database, which knows all 343 editions rather than English alone. Back to the findings.

First, a correction

Their categories were built from Wikipedia category membership, which sweeps in topics that have no structural link to Uganda at all. Every topic here was re-checked against Wikidata for the test this project’s own worklists use: country, a location inside Uganda, Ugandan citizenship, or birth or death there.
